**Embracing high velocity: Building, learning, and expanding horizons.** 🚀

Lately, my schedule has been running at full capacity, but the momentum is exhilarating. Balancing multi-disciplinary projects, research, and personal growth has reinforced my belief in agile execution and continuous self-reinvention.

Here is a quick snapshot of what I’ve been up to:

**1. Ventures & Applied Research**

 Leading **two university-industry collaboration projects**, bridging research insights into real-world applications.

 Co-founding a cross-border startup with international peers, exploring high-impact entrepreneurial opportunities.

 Currently preparing another paper submission for an upcoming conference, with a focus on Edge AI and interactive technologies.

**2. Where Engineering Meets Experience Design**

 **Physical Computing & Edge AI:** Actively working with Raspberry Pi, containerization (Docker/K8s), Local AI, MCP (Model Context Protocol), Multi-Agent architectures, and RAG pipelines to build intelligent edge systems.

 **Design-Engineering Synergy:** Integrating deep UX methodologies—user research, field studies, and interface design—into technical workflows to ensure what we build is not only technically robust, but genuinely human-centered.

**3. Accelerating the Learning Curve & Staying Disciplined**

 **Steeper Learning Velocity:** My ramp-up time for unfamiliar and complex tech stacks has drastically compressed—from months to under two weeks.

 **Global Readiness & Discipline:** Committing to 8 hours/week of intensive English coaching, picking up German, and maintaining a strict 5-day training routine in the gym.

Pushing past boundaries—between hardware, software, and human experience—is where the real magic happens. Excited for the milestones ahead!
